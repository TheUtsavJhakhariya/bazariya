(01)Returns

Return Policy

At BAZARIYAA, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your order, we're here to help.

(02)Eligibility

Eligibility for Returns

You can return products within 7 days of delivery if:

  • The product is unused, unopened, and in its original packaging
  • The product has manufacturing defects or damage
  • You received the wrong product
  • The product doesn't match the description

(03)Exceptions

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items cannot be returned:

  • Personal care items (towels, bath sponges, etc.) if opened
  • Food storage items if used
  • Items damaged due to misuse or negligence
  • Items without original packaging

(04)Process

Return Process

  1. Contact Us: WhatsApp us at within 7 days of delivery
  2. Provide Details: Share your order details and reason for return
  3. Get Approval: We'll review your request and confirm the return
  4. Return Product: Pack the product securely in original packaging
  5. Pickup/Refund: We'll arrange pickup or provide refund instructions

(05)Refunds

Refund Policy

Once we receive and verify the returned product:

  • Refund Time: Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days
  • Refund Method: Refund will be issued via the same payment method used for purchase
  • Cash on Delivery: For COD orders, refund will be processed via bank transfer or UPI
  • Shipping Charges: Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the product is defective

(06)Exchanges

Exchange Policy

We offer exchanges for:

  • Defective or damaged products
  • Wrong products delivered
  • Size/color mismatches (if applicable)

Exchange requests must be made within 7 days of delivery. The replacement product will be shipped once we receive the original item.

Quick Tips
